About TrueMax: more than a number alone

Updated 20 September 2026 · Published by TrueMax

TrueMax is a browser-based face rating and facial analysis product with planning features for eligible adult members. Its purpose is to make the working visible and help people focus on realistic, changeable presentation.

What we are building

A person should be able to start with a front photograph, add a side only if they want, and understand what the result actually means. The report separates raw measurements, scores and reference percentiles. Coach Max adds planning for eligible adult members.

We do not claim that a score defines attractiveness for everyone, measures personal worth or replaces a qualified professional. Different cameras, lighting, expressions and reference populations affect how a result should be interpreted.

Who publishes these guides, and how to check them

Start with the methodology and sources. It distinguishes published measurement concepts from TrueMax's own calibration and scoring choices. The current repeat-photo evidence is not a clinical validation study, and we do not advertise a fixed error margin.

These pages are published by TrueMax. Product descriptions are checked against the implemented flow and billing catalogue when updated. Scientific references explain their specific measurement context; citing a paper does not mean its authors endorse TrueMax.

No clinician endorsement or medical certification is claimed. When a claim cannot be tied to evidence, it should be qualified or removed rather than presented as fact.

For this guide collection, the review distinguishes three things: the visible points and arithmetic, the model's interpretation of those measurements, and what the current product actually offers. Worked examples are labelled as examples, not customer results. An update date records a content revision, not a new validation study.

Use the measurement guides to check a term, the face-score explanation to understand the output and pricing to check access. If those pages disagree with the app, report the page and feature rather than assuming the more confident wording is correct.

Your face is not a target to perfect

Useful changes can involve grooming, routine and presentation. They do not require an app to invent new bones or a universal ideal. Goal previews are illustrations, not a guarantee that a routine will produce a particular face. Health concerns and treatment decisions belong with an appropriately qualified professional.

Privacy choices should be understandable

Core front-photo measurement is on-device. Optional cloud side placement, feedback and Goal previews each have their own upload boundary. See the short explanation and the complete privacy policy. A no-upload analysis path remains available.
