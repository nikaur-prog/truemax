Start with the question you are actually asking

If you want to know how attractive you are, begin with the face score guide. It explains why a photograph can measure geometry but cannot produce a universal verdict about a person. Lighting, expression, camera distance and culture all affect how a face is perceived.

If you already know what you want to change, use the appearance guide or the glow-up plan. Both favour safe, reversible changes before expensive or permanent ones.

The useful boundary

TrueMax measures visible facial geometry against reference data. It does not measure your worth, personality, health or how every person will respond to you. A score is a structured starting point, not an objective definition of attractiveness.
