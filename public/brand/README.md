# TrueMax logo pack

The mark is a measured face: a centre axis, a central landmark and two mirrored profile contours. It is deliberately simple enough to stay recognisable at 16 px.

## Included

- `truemax-lockup.svg` — primary horizontal logo for light backgrounds
- `truemax-lockup-reverse.svg` — horizontal logo for dark backgrounds
- `truemax-lockup-max.svg` — paid Max-tier treatment
- `truemax-mark.svg` and `truemax-mark-light.svg` — square app/social marks
- `truemax-glyph.svg` — one-colour glyph for masks, engraving and interface use
- PNG exports at 512 px and 1024 px
- `favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-192.png`, and `icon-512.png` in `public/`

## Colour

- Ink: `#141518`
- True green: `#0E7A68`
- Digital mint: `#56E6C7`
- Max gold: `#F2C94C`
- Warm background: `#F4F3EF`

Keep clear space around the mark equal to one quarter of its width. Do not stretch, rotate, recolour individual contour strokes, or put the primary dark lockup on a busy photograph.
