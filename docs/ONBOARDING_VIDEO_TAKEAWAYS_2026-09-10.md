## Best fit for TrueMax

1. **Deliver on the acquisition promise.** A scan-focused post should lead to a
   clear first-scan journey, not a tour of every feature. A routine-focused post
   can explain the plan benefit, but the user still chooses their own goals.
   Do not infer a medical condition or personal insecurity from an ad source.
2. **Make the first useful result understandable.** Show one well-supported
   observation, the measurement behind it and its limits. Resolve placement
   failures and report latency before adding more onboarding steps or features.
3. **Personalise the next action, not every screen.** The implemented local
   Build my plan brief collects the user's goals, existing routine and
   constraints. Max proposes a small plan; the user explicitly selects what to
   add. Nothing starts automatically.
4. **Explain one coherent membership.** State included scans, guest scans,
   coaching and billing terms consistently. Preserve the agreed free experience
   unless a separate, explicit pricing decision changes it. Do not copy delayed
   close controls or make weekly pricing look monthly.
5. **Share a useful result deliberately.** A user-selected card can be easier to
   share than a long report. Sharing is not consent for research, face storage or
   model training. Keep those choices separate and optional.
6. **Measure before expanding.** Existing aggregate events cover completed
   front/side scans, side skips, signup return, report display, plan opening and
   checkout starts. These are stage counts, not linked user cohorts or proof of
   trial-to-paid conversion. Verify their coverage and checkout completion
   independently before claiming an uplift.

## Do not copy blindly

- Thirty onboarding screens, ten variants or a new attribution engine. Start
  with one small hypothesis after reliability work, not ten low-sample tests.
- The stated 40% paid conversion or revenue increases. The transcript does not
  establish the denominator, audience, attribution window or test uncertainty.
- Removing login. TrueMax needs clear account ownership for private scans,
  routines and subscription entitlements. Easier sign-in is a separate task.
- A realistic replacement for Max. An astrology audience's mascot test is not
  evidence against the character the owner has already approved.
- Automatic user-face image generation. Keep morphs opt-in, reviewed and clearly
  illustrative; generated faces are not proof of achievable changes.
- Native external billing based on this interview. Review current platform and
  storefront requirements during the signed-app work, not by copying anecdotes.

## Order and evaluation

First: reviewed calibration evidence and predictable first-scan performance.
Second: validate the real Coach responses and chosen-plan flow.
Third: trial a single clearer first-result explanation, with the same offer and
the same measurement engine. Count successful report viewing and useful next
actions, while watching errors, abandonment and complaints. Do not optimise paid
conversion by hiding uncertainty or making rejection harder.

When the native app exists, Apple provides product-page optimisation with
conversion and confidence reporting. This tests the store listing, not the
in-app onboarding, and does not imply the interview's outcomes transfer to us.
See [Apple's product-page optimisation reporting](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect-analytics/acquisition/product-page-optimization/).

## Product-information corrections made during this build

The catalogue now includes a named 5% niacinamide moisturiser example and its
manufacturer page. This checks identity and the labelled percentage only, not
clinical suitability, score improvement or regional stock.
[Manufacturer information](https://www.theinkeylist.com/products/omega-water-cream).

The old "PanOxyl 2.5% wash" example was replaced by an actual 2.5% gel example.
PanOxyl's cited creamy wash is 4%; Benzac's cited gel is 2.5%. Washes and gels are
not interchangeable instructions. Existing guardian safeguards remain intact.
[PanOxyl label](https://panoxyl.com/acne-products/acne-creamy-wash/),
[Benzac label](https://www.benzac.com/au/product/mild-strength-acne-gel).

Removed the incorrect blanket claim that salicylic washes rinse off before
working. The American Academy of Dermatology includes salicylic cleansers among
options; the app now distinguishes formulations and defers to product directions.
[AAD explanation](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/acne/derm-treat/treat).
