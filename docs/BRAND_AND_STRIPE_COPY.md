# TrueMax core copy

## One-sentence product description

> TrueMax analyzes your face on your device and turns the results into a
> personalized plan to help you improve your appearance and feel more
> confident.

This keeps the useful “glow up” idea but says what the product actually does.
It avoids “the necessary pathway,” which sounds like a guarantee and implies
that there is only one correct route.

## Stripe business profile

Category: **Software**

Description to paste:

> TrueMax is a consumer software app that provides on-device facial analysis
> and personalized appearance and self-improvement guidance through monthly
> subscriptions and optional one-time scan purchases.

This is intentionally factual. It tells Stripe what is sold, how it is
delivered and how customers pay without making medical or guaranteed-outcome
claims.

## Short website alternative

> Understand your face. Build a personalized plan. Track your progress.
