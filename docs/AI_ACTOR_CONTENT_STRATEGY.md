# TrueMax AI-actor content lane

## The repeatable reel

1. **Hook (0–2s):** an AI actor looks under-styled, tired or unsure. On-screen: “He followed the wrong glow-up advice.”
2. **Scan (2–6s):** TrueMax face scan and the real exported score reveal.
3. **Direction (6–10s):** highlight two or three specific, non-medical priorities from the plan.
4. **Transformation (10–15s):** show the same AI actor after applying styling, grooming, skincare and presentation changes.
5. **CTA:** “Scan first. Improve what actually matters.”

## Three formats to rotate

- **Transformation story:** before → TrueMax scan → pathway → after.
- **One-feature lesson:** “Why his haircut was fighting his facial proportions.”
- **Expectation vs measurement:** start with a popular glow-up claim, then show what the scan actually measures.

## Production system

- Generate one consistent actor identity with front, side and talking-head reference shots.
- Create three opening conditions for the same identity: poor lighting/styling, neutral baseline and polished outcome.
- Keep the camera, focal length and head angle consistent between before and after shots so the change does not rely on perspective tricks.
- Feed the finished clips into a reusable vertical edit template for captions, TrueMax screen capture, music and CTA.
- Produce three hook variants per concept; change the opening claim and pacing, not merely the file metadata.

## Trust rules

- Label the character **“AI-generated demonstration”** on-screen. Do not present an AI actor as a real customer or testimonial.
  - This rule is scoped to PUBLISHED VIDEO with an AI actor in it — a before/after story a viewer could read as a
    real customer. It does not extend to the product’s own UI. The landing demo card and the gate strip carry no
    on-picture label: they are plainly a product demo, nobody reads them as a testimonial, and the provenance sits
    in the page’s fine print (“Demo faces are AI-generated” in index.html). Do not add the caption back to them.
- Describe the after image as a visualisation of a pathway, not a guaranteed result.
- Do not imply the scan diagnoses medical or dermatological conditions.
- Avoid humiliating “ugly” language. The stronger TrueMax position is clarity and confidence: poorly presented → intentionally presented.
- Separate instant changes (hair, clothing, posture, lighting) from changes that realistically take weeks or months (skin routine, fitness and dental care).

## Hooks worth testing

- “He didn’t need more glow-up advice. He needed the right order.”
- “We scanned his face before changing anything.”
- “Most glow-up advice would focus on the wrong feature first.”
- “This is what changes when the plan is built around your face.”
- “One scan. Three changes. Completely different presentation.”

## Recommended caption

> AI-generated demonstration using a TrueMax analysis workflow. Results and timelines vary. TrueMax provides cosmetic and presentation guidance, not medical diagnosis.

# The funnel, and which format sits where

Settled 30 August, after looking at what is actually working in the niche. Six
outlier posts pulled from the last 30 days, and the finding that matters is not
that looksmaxxing is alive, it is the follower column:

| Account | Followers | Views | What it was |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| @fakecel.psl | 2.1K | 3.7M | "Rating with @PSL App" |
| @saulmogman | 68.6K | 1.3M | "Analysis at FaceIQ Labs" |
| @iambaleegh | 379 | 286K | before/after transformation |
| @hazel.bp | 4.6K | 206K | "Official Scores With @PSL App" |
| @illegal.coke | 22.6K | 218K | "Scores with @ascendr.app" |
| @notxjinx | 174 | 87.9K | "Official rating with @psl.app" |

A 2.1K-follower account got 3.7 million views by pointing a rating app at a
face. The content is not the creator, it is **the app on screen**. There is no
audience to build first, which is why volume beats polish here.

Every one of those captions names an app. TrueMax is in none of them yet, and
that is the whole gap: distribution, not product.

## Top of funnel: the before and after

The highest-reach format, and the one to make the most of. Two lengths:

- **Short.** The scan, then the glow-up. Nothing else.
- **Long.** The before photos, the before scan, a scroll through what Coach Max
  actually suggested, then the after: the same person glowed up, walking,
  filming themselves, confident.

The long one is the more valuable of the two because the middle section is the
product doing something. It is also the only version that answers "is this real
or is it just an edit", which is the objection that stops the short one.

Runs with an AI actor OR a real person. With an AI actor the on-screen
"AI-generated demonstration" tag is required and non-negotiable: see the trust
rules above. A viewer must never be able to mistake a generated transformation
for a customer's.

## Top and middle: celebrities

Before and after on a face the viewer already knows. High reach because the
subject carries it, and it doubles as proof the measurement is doing something
real, because the audience already has an opinion about the face and can check
ours against it.

No verdict word ever names a person. That rule is in CLAUDE.md and it exists
because "Marlon level" shipped once.

## Middle and bottom: the very attractive face

Scanning someone conventionally attractive and showing the number. This is NOT
top of funnel and should not be treated as it: it converts people who have
already seen us and need conviction that the system measures rather than
flatters. Its job is credibility, not reach.

The strongest version of it is the one where the number is not a 9. A tool that
returns a high score for every attractive face is a mirror; a tool that returns
a 7.4 with a named reason is a measurement.

## Two things the competitor's own users asked them for

From PSL's public launch thread, recorded in full in COMPETITOR_INTEL.md. Both
are things TrueMax already does, which makes them positioning rather than
roadmap:

- *"stop being a psl rater and become a glow up progress coach"* — the parts
  that actually change are skin, body fat and grooming, and that audience is
  much larger than the looksmaxxing crowd. Women want it too.
- *"A random score is entertaining for 30 seconds. Seeing specific areas
  improve over time is what would keep me coming back."*

So the content should lead with the second scan more than the first. A rating
video is the hook; a rating video is also every competitor's entire library.

## What TrueMax has that none of them do

The scan history. Every competitor above sells a rating; a rating is a single
number on a single day and it is the same content forever. TrueMax stores the
second scan, which means it is the only one of them that can post a
before/after where both halves are its own measurement with a date on it.

That is the identity to build the account on: not a face, and not a persona.
The person ninety days in with receipts.
