# Max avatar — v1 master

Max is TrueMax's visual guide: calm, direct and optimistic. The character is
separate from the LLM, billing and floating-assistant code so his expression,
pose and animation can evolve without changing product logic.

## Locked visual identity

- five-lobed cloud head in electric cobalt blue;
- deep navy rounded screen face;
- simple cyan expressions that stay readable at 48 px;
- compact blue body with short rounded limbs;
- cyan illuminated seams and one small emerald chest status light; and
- premium matte 3D finish, never human, medical or baby-like.

The green chest light means Max is available. Future states may shift only the
light and face expression: listening, thinking, speaking, success and quiet.
Do not recolour the shell by plan or emotional state.

## Files

- `/public/brand/max-avatar-v1.png` — transparent production master.
- `/public/brand/max-avatar-v1.webp` — optimized 640 px app asset.
- `/assets/max/max-avatar-chroma-v1.png` — removable-background source, not shipped to users.

The production master is a 1254 × 1254 RGBA PNG. Keep the source untouched;
derive smaller WebP/AVIF assets or animation frames from it.
