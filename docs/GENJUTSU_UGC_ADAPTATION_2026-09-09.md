# GENJUTSU: useful after the master clip is good

Assessment, 9 September 2026. Documentation only. No source footage was uploaded, generation credits spent, provider installed or content published. The [complete user-supplied script](references/GENJUTSU_USER_REFERENCE_2026-09-09.md) is archived separately, verbatim and labelled as unverified promotional material.

## What is verified, and what is not

Higgsfield's official page describes **Motion Transfer**, which rebuilds a scene around a source video's motion, camera and timing, and **Object Swap**, which targets a character, outfit, product, location or object. Its FAQ currently lists 3–30-second reference videos, up to 40 reference images and a displayed credit cost before generation. These are documented capabilities, not an independently measured success rate. [Official GENJUTSU page](https://higgsfield.ai/genjutsu).

The tutorial's claims that every grain, mask and effect remains exact, that no previous model could do this, and that one clip can be reused indefinitely are not established by that documentation or by our own testing. Preservation is a generation objective, not a pixel-level guarantee. Website availability also does not establish access through our current connector; inspect the actual supported operation and quote before a pilot.

## The useful ideas to adopt

- **Quality first:** approve the character stills, then one convincing master clip. A remix is not a dependable repair for poor facial motion or an inconsistent starting face.
- **Separate identity from performance:** maintain a small approved identity reference set and a separate camera/action brief. A multi-angle sheet may help review consistency, but generated alternate angles are not measured anatomy or calibration ground truth.
- **One controlled change:** test a background-only variation first, then an outfit variation if useful. Derive each from the approved master instead of repeatedly re-editing a degraded output. Character recasting is a separate concept, never a before/after of the same person.
- **Natural restraint:** one blink, one small glance or gentle breathing is enough for a five-second shot. Leave typography, real app captures, score cards, transitions and beat cuts in our editor.

The supplied examples are not ready-to-run TrueMax briefs: their starting portrait and character sheet describe different hair and wardrobe, and the long motion script adds hand movement, exposure changes, flare, voice performance and background sounds at once. Resolve reference contradictions before generating. Treat the suggested head-cropping trick as a hypothesis to test, not a universal provider rule. Keep a full-body approval image; use a separate detail reference when needed instead of removing identity review from the workflow.

## A compact brief to test

Proposed creative brief, not a submitted provider prompt:

> Five-second vertical chest-up shot of the approved fictional adult, starting from the approved still. Eye-level phone perspective, unchanged outfit and window-lit room. Gentle breathing, one natural blink and a slight glance toward the lens, ending settled. Subtle handheld drift only. No speech, music, cuts, zoom, exposure change or added text. Keep the approved face, skin texture, hair and proportions consistent.

If speech is wanted later, specify one short approved line, one conversational intention and enough time to deliver it. Review mouth motion and audio together. Do not pack three lines, several gestures and a lighting effect into a five-second pilot.

## Pilot and acceptance

1. Confirm the supported operation, duration and quoted cost. Approve one master and at most two single-variable variants; no automatic twenty-clip batch.
2. Compare the full clips against the master at normal speed and frame by frame, especially turns, blinks, mouth shapes, hand/face overlaps and clip boundaries. Check face drift, flicker, changed body proportions, outfit/prop continuity, background warping and audio continuity when present. Matching poster frames are not enough.
3. Accept only variants whose unchanged elements and timing survive that review. Record master ID, references, exact change, prompt, provider job, cost and approval. Retrying or replacing an anchor invalidates dependent approvals.
4. Carry approved assets into the existing reel builder with order, paired context and disclosure intact. Assemble the reveal on the selected beat; do not generate an in-clip identity transformation or invent higher score-card numbers.

Recommendation: save and use the pipeline idea. Prioritize the approved image library and controlled motion pilot; add GENJUTSU only if a comparison shows it saves useful work without compromising identity or temporal quality.
