Delete your account

Last updated 29 August 2026

The short version

Open TrueMax, tap your account, and choose Delete account. It takes effect immediately and needs nothing from us.

If you cannot reach the app — you have uninstalled it, or you are locked out — email privacy@truemax.app from the address on the account and we will do it for you.

What gets deleted

Everything tied to the account, and that is a shorter list than most apps because most of TrueMax never leaves your phone in the first place:

  • the account itself, and your sign-in;
  • your server-side profile, entitlements, feedback records, Creator League records and other account data;
  • your subscription record. If a subscription is active, TrueMax schedules it to stop before the account is removed so it cannot renew;
  • any side-profile feedback images you chose to share, which are queued for removal and deleted within 30 days.

What was never ours to delete

Your front photograph is not uploaded to us under any circumstance — the measurement engine runs inside your browser, on your own device. Scan history and thumbnails on the device are stored locally. Delete them with the in-app history controls or by clearing TrueMax site data; deleting the app also removes its local data. Neither of those is something we can delete for you, because neither was ever sent to us. See the privacy policy for the full account of what is and is not collected.

How long it takes

Deleting from inside the app is immediate. An emailed request is answered within 30 days and usually much sooner; we will confirm when it is done. Shared side-profile images are removed within 30 days either way, because they are deleted on a scheduled sweep rather than one at a time.

Why there is no form here

A page that took an email address and deleted the matching account would let anyone delete anyone's. Proving that a request came from the person who owns the account is the part that matters, so the email route is handled by a person. Write from the address on the account and it is straightforward.
