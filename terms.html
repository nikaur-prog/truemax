What your score actually is

A TrueMax score is a measurement of geometry, compared against a reference sample of faces. It says where your proportions sit relative to that sample. That is all it says.

It is not a measure of your worth, your attractiveness to any particular person, your health, or your future. It is not a medical or psychological assessment, and it is not a diagnosis. Beauty is not actually reducible to a number, and anyone claiming otherwise, including anyone quoting a TrueMax score at you, is overstating what a measurement can do.

The reference sample is finite and imperfect, our measurement carries real error, and we publish that error rather than hiding it. Two photos of the same face on the same day can differ by around a point.
