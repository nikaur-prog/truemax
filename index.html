Now the side profile

Second of two. Chin projection, jaw angle and facial convexity do not exist in a front photo. They can only be measured from the side, and they are among the strongest numbers on the report. The analysis runs once both views are in.

You will not be able to see this screen. So it takes the photo itself: turn until you hear the countdown start, then hold still. Two beeps, then a higher one is the shutter. Space bar takes it immediately if you would rather not wait.

Afterwards, thirteen points are placed for you to review. If any missed, choose edit and drag only those points before confirming.
