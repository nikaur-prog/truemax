How the seven-day trial works

Eligible accounts can start one seven-day subscription trial. Stripe collects a payment method. Cancel before the trial ends to avoid the subscription charge. The offer and Checkout show the renewal amount and date before you agree.

A trial is not needed to see your first account analysis. The initial analysis and the subscription trial are separate. Existing subscriptions may keep a legacy price; check your own billing page for that amount.

Weekly scans, without surprises

A rolling week means seven days after the scan that used a slot, not a reset every Monday. Unused slots do not accumulate. Scans of other people never move your own progress chart or count as your personal scan.

Paid plans include one personal scan per rolling week. If a free account declines the pathway trial, ongoing free access switches to one scan of someone else per rolling week, without a personal progress pathway. The app shows the next available slot for the account you are using.

Prefer a single extra scan?

An extra in-depth scan is $5.99 for a non-member or $2.99 at the paid-member price. It is a one-time purchase, not a Max subscription or an unlimited-scan plan. A voiced analysis is a separate $2.99 purchase when offered. Eligibility and the final price are shown before payment.

Why Max is adults only

Max includes adult weight, calorie and body-composition planning, so access requires an account aged 18 or over. Starter remains an option for younger eligible users. Adults can optionally add height and weight in the post-signup quiz; adult Max members are asked to complete them for their plan. Those values never enter the facial score.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Sign in, open account Settings and choose Manage billing to use the secure billing portal. Cancelling renewal is different from deleting an account. Check the portal for the date your current access ends, and read the terms before buying.

Billing problem? Contact support@truemax.app. Do not email card details or photographs.
