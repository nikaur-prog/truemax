Logo and brand kit

The mark is a measured face: a centre axis, a central landmark, and two mirrored profile contours. It is the product's claim in one shape, and it is drawn simply enough to survive at 16 pixels.

On a phone

Press and hold any logo below, then choose Save to Photos. It will be in your camera roll, ready to drop into CapCut or InShot as an overlay. The Download buttons save to Files instead, which is what you want for the SVGs and the full pack.

Primary lockup

The default. Use this everywhere unless the background is dark.

Square mark

App icon, profile picture, and the watermark on a reel. This is the one to reach for on a video overlay.

Max

The character. Only ever on surfaces meant for over-18s.

Using it

Keep clear space around the mark equal to a quarter of its width. Do not stretch it, rotate it, recolour the individual contour strokes, or put the dark lockup on a busy photograph. On a video, the square mark in a corner at about 8% of frame width reads without covering a face.
